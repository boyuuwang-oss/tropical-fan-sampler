    top edge of a ring (letter = the ring)环的顶边（字母 = 那个环） bridge桥 closes a loop: exactly the rings’ top edges, and U^tr is their product闭合一个圈：恰好就是各环的顶边，U^tr 是它们的乘积 96 above a bar or on an edge = agreement with U^tr (%)柱上或边上的数 = 与 U^tr 的一致程度（%）

    This is all the sampler draws: a tree of rings and one u per edge. The probability of a draw and the tropical weight U^tr = Π (top edge of each ring) follow from these alone, so no list of diagrams is ever made. The diagram is glued together from the tree only to compute the true U, and with it w = (U^tr/U)^{3/2}·Πu^δ.

    采样器抽的只有这些：一棵环的树，加上每条边一个 u。一个样本的概率和热带权重 U^tr = Π（每个环的顶边）只由它们决定，所以从不需要图的列表。只有在计算真实的 U、从而得到 w = (U^tr/U)^{3/2}·Πu^δ 时，才把树拼成一张具体的图。

    The tropical measure picks diagram G with probability p_G (violet). Its true share of s_L is p_G·E[w | G]/E[w] (amber). The weight w is exactly what turns the violet bar into the amber one.热带测度以概率 p_G 抽中图 G（紫色）；它在 s_L 中的真实份额是 p_G·E[w | G]/E[w]（琥珀色）。权重 w 正是把紫色变成琥珀色的那一步。

    Each run starts at a tropical draw (violet), and annealing moves the chain toward the true measure, so where runs end (teal) drifts toward the true share (amber). The contribution uses the weight W of each run at its end: s_G = P₂·mean(W·[run ends in G]), unbiased for any K.每一轮从一个热带样本出发（紫色），退火把链推向真实测度，所以各轮的终点（青色）向真实份额（琥珀色）靠拢。贡献用每轮终点的权重 W：s_G = P₂·mean(W·[该轮停在 G])，对任何 K 都无偏。

    The space空间

    Every 1PI two-point cubic diagram G with E = 3L−1 edges is one cone, the cube (0,1)ᴱ/Aut G in u = 1 − e^{−α}. Cones meet at the UV apex and are glued where an edge length shrinks to zero. Inside a cone, U^tr is a single monomial in each Hepp sector.

    每张 E = 3L−1 条边的三价 1PI 两点图是一个锥，即 u = 1 − e^{−α} 坐标下的 (0,1)ᴱ/Aut G。所有锥在 UV 顶点相交，并在某条边长缩为零的地方粘合。锥内每个 Hepp 扇区里 U^tr 只是一个单项式。

    μ_δ = Π u^{−δ} · (U^tr)^{−3/2} du / |Aut G|, degree ω_δ = (1−δ)E − 3L/2

    Global tropical全局热带

    Draw the largest edge t first. Cutting it leaves a chain of beads joined by bridges below t, and each bead is a smaller 1PI graph drawn the same way one scale down. The draw is exact from μ_δ, so no list of diagrams is ever needed. Then reweight by w ≤ 1.

    先抽最大的边 t。剪开它，剩下一条由桥连起的珠子链，桥都小于 t；每个珠子是更小的 1PI 图，在下一个尺度上用同样的规则抽。抽样精确服从 μ_δ，不需要图的列表。最后用 w ≤ 1 加权。

    ω_δ(n,L) P_n(L) = ½ ch(n, L−1), ŝ_L = P₂(L) · mean(w)

    Annealed importance sampling退火重要性采样

    Start from one exact tropical draw and raise β from 0 to 1 in K steps, banking Δβ·log w at each step. In between, Markov moves leave μ_δ·w^β invariant: regrow a whole subtree from the exact tropical rule (a jump to another wedge), or redraw one edge (same wedge). Accept with (w′/w)^β.

    从一个精确的热带样本出发，用 K 步把 β 从 0 升到 1，每步记下 Δβ·log w。中间用保持 μ_δ·w^β 不变的马尔可夫移动：按精确热带规则重长一整棵子树（跳到另一个楔形），或重抽一条边（留在同一楔形）。接受率 (w′/w)^β。

    E[W] = s_L / P₂(L) for every K, Var log W ≈ V_f + c/K

    This page runs a JavaScript port of the global tropical sampler (exact tables, top-edge generator, Kron reduction for U) and of the annealed-importance-sampling kernel (subtree regrowth, bridge and top-edge redraws) described in I. Moult and B. Wang, “Instanton Structure from 325-Loop Perturbation in 3D φ³ Theory”. It is a teaching copy, not the production code. Reference values s_L are those tabulated in the paper (exact for L = 1, 2). Diagrams are identified up to isomorphism by a colour-refinement hash with the two external vertices marked; above 24 diagrams the fan pools them into 24 bins, while the share table still lists them one by one. The angle inside a wedge is the mean of u/t over the edges, a one-number summary of the diagram's shape.
