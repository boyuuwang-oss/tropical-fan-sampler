    5. Each Next draws one edge, largest first. Among the edges still left, edge e is the largest with probability $\frac{J(γ∖e)}{ω(γ∖e)\,J(γ)}$, read off the table $J$ over all $2^E$ edge subsets of this one diagram. The first edge gets $α = 1$; each later one is smaller by a factor $ξ^{1/ω(γ)}$ with $ξ$ uniform on $(0,1)$. Violet means the edge closes a loop of what is left, so its $α$ is a factor of $U^{tr}$; gray means it is a bridge of what is left. The order of all $E$ edges is the Hepp sector.每按一次“下一步”画一条边，从大到小。在剩下的边里，边 e 是最大的概率为 $\frac{J(γ∖e)}{ω(γ∖e)\,J(γ)}$，查自这张图全部 $2^E$ 个边子集上的表 $J$。第一条边取 $α = 1$；之后每条边再乘上一个因子 $ξ^{1/ω(γ)}$（$ξ$ 在 $(0,1)$ 上均匀分布）。紫色表示这条边闭合剩余部分的一个圈，它的 $α$ 是 $U^{tr}$ 的因子；灰色表示它是剩余部分的桥。全部 $E$ 条边的顺序就是 Hepp 扇区。
    6. The price is the table. $J$ has $2^E$ entries for every diagram: $2^{20} ≈ 10^6$ at $L = 7$, $2^{29} ≈ 5×10^8$ at $L = 10$, and the number of diagrams grows factorially. That is why this mode stops at $L = 7$, and why the paper moves on to global tropical sampling, which never lists diagrams.代价是这张表。每张图的 $J$ 有 $2^E$ 项：$L = 7$ 时 $2^{20} ≈ 10^6$，$L = 10$ 时 $2^{29} ≈ 5×10^8$，而图的数目按阶乘增长。所以这个模式只到 $L = 7$，论文也因此转向从不列举图的全局热带采样。
    7. Each Next is one step of the annealing chain. Raise $β$ a little and bank $Δβ\,\text{log}\,w$, or make one move: regrow a ring and everything inside it from the exact tropical rule (teal if accepted, coral if rejected), or redraw one edge. After $K$ steps of $β$ the run ends and its weight $W$ lands on the fan.每按一次“下一步”是退火链的一步。把 $β$ 升高一点并记下 $Δβ\,\text{log}\,w$，或者做一次移动：按精确的热带规则重长一个环和它里面的一切（被接受为青色，被拒绝为珊瑚色），或者重抽一条边。$β$ 走完 $K$ 步后这一轮结束，它的权重 $W$ 落到扇上。

    This is all the sampler draws: a tree of rings and one $u$ per edge. The probability of a draw and the tropical weight $U^{tr} = Π\,(\text{top edge of each ring})$ follow from these alone, so no list of diagrams is ever made. The diagram is glued together from the tree only to compute the true $U$, and with it $w = {(\frac{U^{tr}}{U})}^{3/2}\,Π_e\,u_e^{δ}$.

    采样器抽的只有这些：一棵环的树，加上每条边一个 $u$。一个样本的概率和热带权重 $U^{tr} = Π$（每个环的顶边）只由它们决定，所以从不需要图的列表。只有在计算真实的 $U$、从而得到 $w = {(\frac{U^{tr}}{U})}^{3/2}\,Π_e\,u_e^{δ}$ 时，才把树拼成一张具体的图。

    This is all diagram tropical draws: an order of the $E$ edges (the Hepp sector) and $E−1$ ratios. In that sector $U^{tr}$ is the product of the violet $α$’s, so $w = {(\frac{U^{tr}}{U})}^{3/2}(Σ_e α_e)^{−ω} ≤ 1$, and $s_G = \frac{Γ(ω)\,J(G)}{|\text{Aut}\,G|}\,\text{mean}(w)$. It is exact and precise for one diagram, but it needs the whole table $J$ of this diagram first, and every diagram must be done separately.

    单图热带抽的只有这些：$E$ 条边的一个顺序（Hepp 扇区）和 $E−1$ 个比值。在这个扇区里 $U^{tr}$ 是紫色各 $α$ 的乘积，所以 $w = {(\frac{U^{tr}}{U})}^{3/2}(Σ_e α_e)^{−ω} ≤ 1$，$s_G = \frac{Γ(ω)\,J(G)}{|\text{Aut}\,G|}\,\text{mean}(w)$。对一张图它精确而且精度高，但先要算出这张图的整张表 $J$，而且每张图都得单独做一遍。

    The space空间

    Every 1PI two-point cubic diagram $G$ with $E = 3L−1$ edges is one cone, the cube $(0,1)^E/\text{Aut}\,G$ in $u = 1 − e^{−α}$. Cones meet at the UV apex and are glued where an edge length shrinks to zero. Inside a cone, $U^{tr}$ is a single monomial in each Hepp sector.

    每张 $E = 3L−1$ 条边的三价 1PI 两点图是一个锥，即 $u = 1 − e^{−α}$ 坐标下的 $(0,1)^E/\text{Aut}\,G$。所有锥在 UV 顶点相交，并在某条边长缩为零的地方粘合。锥内每个 Hepp 扇区里 $U^{tr}$ 只是一个单项式。

    $μ_δ = \frac{Π_e\,u_e^{−δ}\,du_e}{(U^{tr})^{3/2}\,|\text{Aut}\,G|}, \quad ω_δ = (1−δ)E − \frac{3L}{2}$

    Diagram tropical单图热带

    Borinsky’s method integrates one diagram at a time. In each Hepp sector, an ordering of the $α$’s, $U$ is replaced by its largest monomial $U^{tr}$, which can be integrated exactly. A recursion over all $2^E$ edge subsets gives the total $J(G)$ and draws a sector edge by edge, largest first; the ratios follow from uniform numbers. The weight $w ≤ 1$ corrects for $U^{tr} ≤ U$.

    Borinsky 的方法一次积分一张图。在每个 Hepp 扇区（$α$ 的一种排序）里，把 $U$ 换成它最大的单项式 $U^{tr}$，后者可以精确积分。对全部 $2^E$ 个边子集的递推给出总量 $J(G)$，并从大到小逐条边抽出一个扇区；比值由均匀随机数得到。权重 $w ≤ 1$ 修正 $U^{tr} ≤ U$ 的差别。

    $J(γ) = Σ_{e∈γ} \frac{J(γ∖e)}{ω(γ∖e)}, \quad ω(γ) = |γ| − \frac{3}{2}L(γ), \quad s_G = \frac{Γ(ω)\,J(G)}{|\text{Aut}\,G|}\,E[w]$

    Global tropical全局热带

    Draw the largest edge $t$ first. Cutting it leaves a chain of beads joined by bridges below $t$, and each bead is a smaller 1PI graph drawn the same way one scale down. The draw is exact from $μ_δ$, so no list of diagrams is ever needed. Then reweight by $w ≤ 1$.

    先抽最大的边 $t$。剪开它，剩下一条由桥连起的珠子链，桥都小于 $t$；每个珠子是更小的 1PI 图，在下一个尺度上用同样的规则抽。抽样精确服从 $μ_δ$，不需要图的列表。最后用 $w ≤ 1$ 加权。

    $ω_δ(n,L)\,P_n(L) = \frac{1}{2}\,\text{ch}(n, L−1), \quad ŝ_L = P_2(L)\,\text{mean}(w)$

    Annealed tropical退火热带

    Start from one exact tropical draw and raise $β$ from 0 to 1 in $K$ steps, banking $Δβ\,\text{log}\,w$ at each step. In between, Markov moves leave $μ_δ\,w^β$ invariant: regrow a whole subtree from the exact tropical rule (a jump to another wedge), or redraw one edge (same wedge). Accept with $(w′/w)^β$.

    从一个精确的热带样本出发，用 $K$ 步把 $β$ 从 0 升到 1，每步记下 $Δβ\,\text{log}\,w$。中间用保持 $μ_δ\,w^β$ 不变的马尔可夫移动：按精确热带规则重长一整棵子树（跳到另一个楔形），或重抽一条边（留在同一楔形）。接受率 $(w′/w)^β$。

    $E[W] = \frac{s_L}{P_2(L)}\ \text{for every}\ K, \quad \text{Var}\,\text{log}\,W ≈ V_f + \frac{c}{K}$

Wang, “Instanton Structure from 325-Loop 3D φ³ Perturbation Theory”: diagram tropical sampling of one diagram (the table $J$ over its $2^E$ edge subsets), the global tropical sampler (exact tables, top-edge generator, Kron reduction for $U$) and the annealed tropical sampler (annealed importance sampling with subtree regrowth, bridge and top-edge redraws). It is a teaching copy, not the production code. Reference values $s_L$ are those tabulated in the paper (exact for $L = 1, 2$). Diagrams are identified up to isomorphism, with the two external legs treated as a pair, by a canonical form (colour refinement with edge multiplicities, plus individualization). The fan is a schematic: each global or annealed draw is placed in one of five cones by its diagram number, and its angle inside the cone summarizes the diagram’s shape (the mean of $u/t$ over its edges). In diagram tropical mode the sector map is exact.
